Dependencies installed later
----------------------------
The GUI downloads PyTorch, NumPy, safetensors, comfy-kitchen and packaging
into a local virtual environment when the user chooses installation. The
comfy-kitchen package is its official pure Python wheel. These packages are
not bundled in the initial installer. Their own licenses apply.
